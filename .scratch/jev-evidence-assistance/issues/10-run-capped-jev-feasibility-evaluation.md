## Hard dispatch budget

At most 20 dispatched provider attempts in total, with at most 10 development attempts and at most 10 held-out evaluation attempts. Keep the US$2 TypeSafe spending target. Failed, timed-out, malformed and uncertain dispatched outcomes consume attempts. Stop if accounting becomes unknown or the spending target would be exceeded. Disable SDK retries and automatic reruns. No repair call, replacement failure, capture or repetition is exempt from these counts. Unused development allowance cannot increase the ten-attempt evaluation limit.

Reserve the planned full-run count before starting each run and keep a durable, content-free attempt ledger so interruption cannot reset the budget. Respect applicable account and service limits; do not rotate identities or keys to gain allowance. An abort does not establish remote cancellation or a refund.

- Executed exactly 20 provider attempts, ten development and ten held-out, across eight source documents and one development probe.
