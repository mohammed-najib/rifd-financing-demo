# 14: Run focused acceptance and decide activation readiness

**What to build:** The operator receives one bounded acceptance report showing what the experimental assistance actually does, what failed or was skipped, and whether its concrete behavior and operational controls are ready for activation.

**Blocked by:** [13: Prepare and verify hosted operation](13-prepare-and-verify-hosted-operation.md).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

Specification: [Experimental Jev evidence assistance](../../jev-evidence-assistance/spec.md). Relevant decisions: [Focused acceptance and honest limits](../../jev-evidence-assistance/spec.md#focused-acceptance-and-honest-limits); [What has been measured](../../jev-evidence-assistance/spec.md#what-has-been-measured); [Server, accounts, spending and privacy](../../jev-evidence-assistance/spec.md#server-accounts-spending-and-privacy).

User stories: 24, 25, 38, 43, 62, 64.

- [ ] Run one shared ten-minute elapsed batch covering preparation, focused checks, source inspection and reporting. Reserve the final minute for shutdown/reporting and do not start work that cannot finish in time. Do not chain batches or recreate the retired validation experiment.
- [ ] Run the existing focused offline regressions for malformed/null Box, original-boundary clipping, competing/repeated values, target/crop mismatch, partial coverage, period ordering, stale results and unavailable stages. Include fake four-page composition and spending/admission stops; add no broad matrix or synthetic calibration search.
- [ ] Before any paid dispatch, recheck reconciliation of the two historical outstanding attempts and current actual credit, then reserve under the US$1 cumulative actual pre-feature validation ceiling and US$4 protected reconciled credit. Count pessimistic outstanding reservations and unknown/failed outcomes; unresolved accounting prevents paid work.
- [ ] Only if accounting and remaining time permit, check at most two known fictional one-page examples, one Arabic and one English, with at most one combined request and one bound-verification request each. The maximum is four new attempts total including failures, unknown outcomes and captures, with no automatic retries or replacement calls.
- [ ] Inspect displayed findings and regions against the actual sources. Record output, suppression and failure counts; preparation, provider and end-to-end timings; actual available usage/billing evidence; and passes, failures, skips and incomplete work. Zero output and a skipped native check must remain explicit.
- [ ] Preserve evidence of the stopped controller, original pilot failures, unchanged checked Saved demonstrations and all historical policy versions. The known examples are exposed smoke/regression inputs, not fresh held-out evaluation; no retired selector, signed experiment freeze or five-family eligibility process is required.
- [ ] Block a concretely misleading finding, unsafe region, broken source binding, permission leak or spending-control failure until fixed or explicitly suppressed. Suppression remains visible and cannot count as successful capability coverage. Report a problem exceeding the small scope for a human decision without extending collection or silently removing a capability.
- [ ] Record readiness against the implemented operational controls and scoped outcomes, keeping live dispatch disabled while concrete prerequisites or unresolved faulty behavior remain. If skipped work leaves readiness unestablished, report that limitation without treating the skip as a pass.
- [ ] Retain the experimental wording and unverified claims: broad bilingual accuracy/calibration, harm frequency, all slot/outcome behavior, native four-page reliability, deployed/mobile p95 and human time savings. The desired quality, usefulness, US$0.01/US$0.02 cost and 12/35-second p95 targets are not measurements or a new statistical-validation prerequisite.
