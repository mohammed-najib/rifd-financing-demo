# Financing application and review

Terms for the fictional financing application journey shared by applicants and reviewers.

## Language

**Application**:
A request by an applicant for a financing product, including their supplied information and supporting documents.
_Avoid_: Case, loan

**Applicant**:
The person who submits financing applications and supplies any requested corrections. An applicant can have multiple applications, each belonging to that applicant.
_Avoid_: Borrower, client

**Reviewer**:
A staff member who examines an application, requests corrections, and records a financing decision.
_Avoid_: Approver, agent

**Application status**:
The stage of an application's journey, distinct from its documents' review outcomes, correction requests, and reviewer assignment.
_Avoid_: Document status, assignment status

**Draft**:
An application the applicant is preparing and has not submitted for review.

**Submitted**:
An application handed over for review whose review has not yet started.

**Under review**:
An application in the reviewer examination stage, including an application returned after corrections.

**Corrections requested**:
An application waiting for the applicant to respond to a reviewer's request for changes.
_Avoid_: Rejected, returned

**Approved**:
An application with a final positive financing decision.
_Avoid_: Paid, disbursed

**Rejected**:
An application with a final negative financing decision.
_Avoid_: Corrections requested, cancelled

**Correction request**:
A reviewer's request for specific changes to an application's information or supporting documents, with reasons for those changes.

**Correction round**:
One cycle of requested corrections, the applicant's resubmission, and the reviewer's verification of the response.

**Assigned reviewer**:
The reviewer responsible for an application's review.
_Avoid_: Application owner, applicant

**Reviewer assignment**:
The designation of the reviewer responsible for an application, independent of its application status and review outcomes.
_Avoid_: Review start, application status

**Reviewer reassignment**:
A transfer of responsibility from one reviewer to another that preserves the application's status and completed review outcomes.
_Avoid_: Review restart, resubmission

**Demo identity**:
A selectable fictional applicant or reviewer whose role determines the application information and actions available in the demonstration.
_Avoid_: Verified identity, bank account

**Correction target**:
A specific application field or document slot the reviewer has asked the applicant to change, with a reason.

**Correction baseline**:
The application's information and documents at the moment a correction request was issued, used to identify changes in the response.
_Avoid_: Original application, previous draft

**Submitted snapshot**:
The applicant information and attachments handed over at submission or resubmission and available for reviewer assessment.
_Avoid_: Partial correction response

**Correction workspace**:
The applicant's saved, unfinished changes being prepared in response to the current correction request.
_Avoid_: Submitted snapshot

**Document slot**:
A place for a supporting document that fulfills a particular application requirement.
_Avoid_: Upload, attachment file

**Document review status**:
The review outcome or outstanding review need for a document slot, independent of application status and upload progress.

**Missing**:
A document slot with no supplied document.

**Awaiting review**:
A document slot with a supplied document that the reviewer has not yet assessed.

**Accepted**:
A document slot whose supplied document the reviewer has found suitable.
_Avoid_: Approved

**Needs replacement**:
A document slot whose supplied document the reviewer has found unsuitable, with a reason explaining the required replacement.
_Avoid_: Rejected

**Applicant history**:
The sequence of an application's submission, review start, correction requests, resubmissions, and final decision visible to its applicant.

**Internal activity**:
Staff activity associated with an application, including assignment changes, document assessments, checklist edits, and internal notes.

**Internal note**:
A staff-only comment added to an application by its assigned reviewer, separate from applicant-visible correction and decision reasons.
_Avoid_: Decision reason, applicant message

**Applicant notification**:
A notice of a correction request or final decision that directs the applicant to the relevant application.

**Resubmission**:
The applicant's handoff of completed requested changes for reviewer verification.
_Avoid_: Approval, acceptance

**Correction verification**:
A reviewer's assessment that a requested field change or document addition or replacement satisfies its correction target.

**Review check**:
A required examination of an application's information, documents, or correction responses before a financing decision.

**Review criterion**:
A substantive eligibility or suitability condition used to judge a financing application.
_Avoid_: Upload validation, document presence

**Decision reason**:
The applicant-visible explanation for an application's final approval or rejection.
_Avoid_: Internal note

**Repayment overview**:
An explicitly fictional illustration of repayment entries associated with an approved application's financing terms.
_Avoid_: Payment processing, financing decision

**Financing product**:
A fictional financing option with its own purpose, amount and term limits, document requirements, and illustrative markup.
_Avoid_: Bank offering

**Inventory financing / تمويل المخزون**:
A fictional product for an individual owner's established small business to replenish stock, with smaller financing amounts and shorter repayment terms.

**Equipment financing / تمويل المعدات**:
A fictional product for an individual owner's established small business to purchase productive equipment, with larger financing amounts and longer repayment terms.

**Financing amount**:
The amount the applicant requests before adding the demo's illustrative markup.
_Avoid_: Repayment total

**Illustrative markup**:
A fictional additional amount applied once to the financing amount when illustrating repayment.
_Avoid_: Bank rate, actual financing charge

**Repayment term**:
The number of months over which the approved application's illustrated installments are scheduled.

**Repayment total**:
The financing amount plus the illustrative markup, distributed across the repayment schedule.
_Avoid_: Financing amount

**Purchase budget**:
The total expected cost of the stock or equipment purchase, which may be partly funded by the requested financing amount.
_Avoid_: Financing amount, repayment total

**Average monthly revenue**:
The business's average monthly revenue over the three completed months before the application's initial submission month.
_Avoid_: Monthly surplus

**Average monthly operating expenses**:
The business's average monthly operating costs over the three completed months before initial submission, before repayments illustrated for this application.
_Avoid_: Repayment installment

**Revenue and expense summary**:
Supporting evidence for the application's declared average monthly business revenue and operating expenses.

**Stock purchase plan**:
Supporting evidence describing the inventory purchase and its expected total cost.

**Supplier quotation**:
Supporting evidence describing the equipment purchase and its quoted total cost.

**Monthly surplus**:
Average monthly revenue minus average monthly operating expenses, before the application's illustrated repayments.
_Avoid_: Monthly revenue, repayment total

**Operating history**:
The number of operating months between the business's declared start month and the application's initial submission month.

**Product purpose fit**:
The review assessment that the intended purchase serves the purpose of the selected financing product.

**Purchase budget coverage**:
The review assessment that the requested financing amount does not exceed the purchase budget.

**Repayment capacity**:
The fictional review assessment comparing the largest illustrated monthly installment with the business's monthly surplus.
_Avoid_: Credit score

**Evidence consistency**:
The review assessment that accepted supporting documents agree with the application's current names, business details, financial figures, and intended purchase.

**Applicant identity evidence**:
A supporting document naming the applicant whose financing request is under review.

**Business evidence**:
A supporting document describing the business's name, address, and operating-since month.

**Repayment installment**:
One scheduled portion of the fictional repayment total, with an amount, due date, and completion status.
_Avoid_: Financing decision

**Repayment schedule**:
The ordered installments of an approved application's fictional repayment illustration, fixed from its accepted terms and approval date.
_Avoid_: Live payment account

**Upcoming installment**:
A scheduled installment still outstanding in the fictional repayment illustration.

**Completed installment**:
An installment recorded as completed in the fictional repayment illustration.
